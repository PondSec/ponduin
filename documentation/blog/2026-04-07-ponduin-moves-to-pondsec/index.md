Ponduin is the local AI agent by [PondSec](https://pondsec.com), built for
privacy, autonomy and professional automation.

<!-- truncate -->

## Local by design

Ponduin operates on your machine and gives you direct control over the models,
tools, files and integrations available to the agent. It supports coding,
terminal workflows, file management and repeatable multi-step automation.

## One precise product identity

The desktop application, command-line interface, documentation, configuration
namespace and release artifacts consistently use the Ponduin name.

- Product: **Ponduin**
- CLI: `ponduin`
- Website: [ponduin.de](https://ponduin.de)
- Company: [PondSec](https://pondsec.com)

## Privacy first

Ponduin is designed around local control. Provider choice remains explicit, tool
permissions remain visible and local workflows stay under the user's control.

Visit the [Ponduin documentation](https://ponduin.de/docs) to get started.
